# 10. Presentation

Prepare a short (~15 min) summary of your work for the CMSDAS symposium.

Suggested outline:
1. **Goal** — Z→ττ cross-section in μτ_h with 2024 data; TauID + TES scale factors; tagger comparison.
2. **Samples & selection** — 2024 Muon data + DY/tt/W/ST/VV MC; the μτ_h selection; the flat n-tuples.
3. **Control plots** — data/MC agreement; the OS/SS data-driven QCD.
4. **Tagger comparison** — the ROC of DeepTau2018v2p5 / PNet / UParT; which you chose and why.
5. **TauID + TES** — the measured scale factors (Tier A applied at plot level; Tier B Combine fit) and
   their effect.
6. **Cross-section** — σ(Z→ττ) with its uncertainty breakdown (from impacts); the σ(Z→ττ)/σ(Z→μμ)
   cross-check.
7. **Conclusions & lessons learned.**

Tips: show a few well-chosen plots (before/after corrections, the ROC, the impacts, the final result);
state numbers with uncertainties; be ready to explain the QCD estimate and the biggest systematic.
